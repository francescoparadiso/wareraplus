**The Numbers**

Masovia, three rounds, finished: **682,005,729** damage from the attack, **665,855,765** from the defence, **1,347,861,494** in total. That was the ceiling of this game for five weeks.

Central Ukraine, two rounds, fourteen hours and sixteen minutes: **1,141,566,248** from the attack, **1,003,811,252** from the defence, **2,145,377,500** in total.

**59% more damage, in one round fewer.** And the detail that says it best: the **losing** side of this battle put up **1.004 billion** on its own - more than either coalition of the old record managed across all three of its rounds. Ukraine lost this region while out-damaging every army that has ever won one.

Between them the two sides landed **675,367 hits**: 357,265 from the attack, 318,102 from the defence.

To put it another way: round one of Central Ukraine - the 1.161 billion we wrote about this afternoon - was on its own **86% of the entire Masovia record**. Round two added another **984 million** on top of it.

**The Bill: A Quarter of a Million, and Falling Rates**

Earlier today we reported 172,613 spent in round one. The final bill is bigger, and the way it grew says more than the total.

Bounty paid out: **80,453** from the attacking pool, **75,201** from the defending one - **155,654** distributed to the people pulling triggers. Mercenary contracts awarded: **371**, worth **106,042**. Lithuania bought **173** of them for **58,926**; Ukraine bought **198** for **47,116** - still more contracts than the aggressor, still less money behind each one.

**Total war spending on this single region: 261,696.**

And none of it moved in the final hours. Both bounty pools ran dry with the round still open, and not a single new mercenary contract was posted after that: the last stretch of the largest battle in this game's history was fought **for free**, by people who had already been paid everything there was to pay.

And now the detail that decided it. **Both sides cut their bounty rate mid-fight.** Lithuania went from **0.1** per thousand damage to **0.08**. Ukraine went from **0.086** to **0.05** - a **42% pay cut** to its own defenders, applied at 18:46 UTC, in the middle of the round it had to win. The Ukrainian bounty pool ended the battle at **-1.05**: not low, *overdrawn*.
