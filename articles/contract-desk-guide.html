2 Step by step

🪖 Unit commanders

1
Press “Request a contract” — or type /contract
Choose the battle & side from the menu (or search the battle as you type).
2
Fill the form
Minimum damage (1m, 500k), bounty per 1,000 damage (0.08), burst damage = what you can hit right away (optional), professionals only, a note.
3
Wait for the government
They are tagged at once and see the whole queue under your request. Your desk shows how long you have been waiting.
4
Watch the result
You get Won or Lost in your unit's channel. If another unit takes the auction, the government is alerted too.

🏛️ Government

1
You are tagged
President, VP and everyone with the manage mercenary contracts right.
2
Approve or Reject
On the request card, via Pending on the desk, or from the menu under /contract-queue nation. Same result as on the website.
3
Open the auction in game
Then press “I opened the auction” so the bot can watch it.
4
The bot checks the outcome
Won, stolen by another unit, different terms, or no bid — shown on the desk.

Setting up a channel (government or unit commander): type /contract-desk post and pick My nation or My military unit. The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links there. Use /contract-desk remove to stop.
Check the queue any time (only you see the answer): /contract-queue unit — commanders: your requests, your place in line and how long that government usually takes to decide. /contract-queue nation — government: the whole queue, oldest first, 🔥 for anyone waiting over 30 minutes.
Not getting tagged? Type /register first (see the yellow box).
